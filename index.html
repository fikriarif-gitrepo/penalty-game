Penalty Shootout Rules

  1. A coin toss decides which team kicks first.
  2. Teams take kicks alternately (A-B-A-B).
  3. Each team takes 5 kicks. If one team can no longer catch up, the shootout ends early.
  4. If still level after 5 kicks each, it goes to sudden death: one kick each until one team scores and the other doesn't.
  5. The ball is placed on the penalty mark (11 m from goal).
  6. The goalkeeper must stay on the goal line between the posts until the ball is kicked.
  7. The kicker kicks the ball forward once. No rebounds or follow-ups.
  8. A goal counts only when the whole ball crosses the line between the posts and under the crossbar. Off the post and in counts. Off the post and out does not.

Controls

  • Shooting: tap or click where you want to shoot, then tap again to stop the power bar. Aim for the green zone. If you hit red, the ball can fly over the bar.
  • Goalkeeping: while the kicker runs up, tap where you want to dive. Tap again to change. If you don't tap, you stay in the middle.
  • Keyboard: Space or Enter stops the power bar.

Arcade version: you take all of your team's kicks and make all of its saves.
